Before you begin
1
Connect the ESP32-C3 SuperMini to this computer with a USB data cable.
2
Pick a version below, click Install Firmware and select the serial port when the browser asks.
3
The installer then asks whether to erase the device:
Leave unticked
Flash and keep settings. For updates: Wi-Fi, CPU family, base clock and your presets stay. The web pages are replaced.
✓Tick "Erase device"
Flash and wipe all data. For the first install, or a board that ran other firmware. Export your presets first.
4
After a fresh install, join the HAWK370-Setup Wi-Fi hotspot (password hawk370setup) and open hawk370.local (or 192.168.8.1) to run the setup wizard. After an update the dashboard is back at its usual address.
